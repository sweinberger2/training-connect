Your assistant wants to connect to your Training account. It will be able to:

  • Read today's meals, what's logged, and your calorie and protein totals
  • Search your foods and the food list
  • Log a meal or an extra for today or yesterday, with calories worked out by Training
  • Undo entries it made

It can't see your weight, health data or history beyond yesterday, change your plan or settings, or delete anything else. Entries it makes are marked and can be undone in the app. You can disconnect any time in Training → Profile.
